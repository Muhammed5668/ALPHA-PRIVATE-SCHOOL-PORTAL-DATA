App Store & Play Store Roadmap

1️⃣ Convert to Native Hybrid Bundle

Use **CapacitorJS** or **Apache Cordova** to wrap this web application. This bundles your HTML, CSS, and JS into a compiled native wrapper for iOS (Swift/Xcode) and Android (Kotlin/Gradle).

2️⃣ Register Store Developer Accounts
  • **Apple Developer Program:** $99/year (Requires D-U-N-S Number for institutional registration).
  • **Google Play Console:** $25 one-time registration fee.
3️⃣ Prepare Launch Assets

Prepare app launcher icons (1024x1024 px), the splash screens (which will load instantly using our CSS animations), and device mockups showing active panels.

4️⃣ Submit for Store Review

Compile the release build (`.ipa` for iOS and `.aab` bundle for Android), fill out App Store Privacy cards, and submit for verification (typically takes 24-72 hours).
